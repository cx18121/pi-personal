# Performance

Measure the workload and outcome that matter to the consumer. Establish a comparable baseline and enough normal variance to distinguish an improvement from noise.

Use traces, profiles, query plans, counters, or focused instrumentation to locate the cost. Form a falsifiable explanation of how a change should improve the measured path. Separate changes when combining them would obscure the cause of the result.

Compare equivalent before and after workloads. Account for correctness and costs displaced into other paths. Report the measured improvement and its limits. A faster proxy does not establish a faster user experience.
